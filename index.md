## Prerequisites

This lesson assumes you have a working knowledge of R and some previous exposure to the Bash shell.
These requirements can be fulfilled by:

1. Completing a Software Carpentry R workshop **or**
1. Independent exposure to both R and the Bash shell.

If you're unsure whether you have enough experience to participate in this workshop, please read over
[this detailed list](learners/prereqs.md), which gives all of the functions, operators, and other concepts you will need
to be familiar with.
